5Stores, outfit screen, sharing, Basket + Wearing screens
Follows on from round 4. There are now two lists: Wearing (what's on your avatar) and Basket (things you mean to buy). Each card shows an orange checkroom badge if you're wearing it and a white basket badge if it's in your basket. The card menu is Try · Basket · Buy · View.
Breadcrumbs appear once you go deeper than a tab (e.g. Outfits › Neon Rider › Varsity Jacket). Tap any crumb to jump back. Price is now a min/max filter. Stores are a vertical column of horizontal sections: an item viewer, carousels and collections. Stores and outfits each have a share code you reveal by tapping. It comes with a copy box and a QR that opens the same screen in the game.
Get to Basket from the basket button bottom-left (HUD) or the basket card in any screen. Get to Wearing by tapping the avatar preview, or your avatar in the world.
